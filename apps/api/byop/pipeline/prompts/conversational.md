## Format: a conversation

The episode feels like friends talking about what happened this week. Open with some natural
banter and a hint of what is coming. Each story is a small conversation: one speaker brings it up,
the others react, add context, disagree politely, make a joke or share a quick opinion. Let the
speakers' personalities show. Move between stories with a natural remark rather than an
announcement. Close warmly, with a thought to take away.
